1. WHO WE SERVE - PEOPLE AND BENEFICIARIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 SC population ..................... 20.14 crore (16.6% of India)          [Fact]
      Source: Census 2011, via MoSJE Handbook on Social Welfare Statistics 2021
1.2 SCs living in rural areas ......... 76.4%  -> 15.39 crore                  [Fact + Estimate]
    SCs in urban areas ................ 23.6%  ->  4.75 crore
1.3 SC literacy ....................... 66.1%  -> about 34% cannot read       [Fact + Estimate]
      Source: Census 2011 via MoSJE Handbook 2021
1.4 SCs below the poverty line (the group eligible for GIA income & skill help)
      Official poverty ratios 2011-12 (Tendulkar method, Planning Commission,
      reported in Lok Sabha): SC rural 31.5%, SC urban 21.7%            [Fact]
      15.39 cr x 31.5% = 4.85 cr   +   4.75 cr x 21.7% = 1.03 cr
      = about 5.9 crore SC people below the poverty line                [Estimate]
      Note: poverty has fallen since 2011-12, so this is an upper-end pool; it
      is the latest official poverty data split by social group.
1.5 Who is eligible: "Scheduled Castes persons living below the poverty lines are
      eligible ... under Income Generating Schemes and Skill Development
      Programmes"; beneficiaries are selected through Panchayati Raj
      institutions.                                       [Fact, PIB explainer, 1 May 2025]
1.6 GIA (Grant-in-aid) delivery so far: since 2021-22, 9,549 projects approved,
      Rs 1,219.80 crore released, 2,01,006 SC beneficiaries.
                                   [Fact, PIB 19 Mar 2025, Rajya Sabha US Q2194, PRID 2112697]
      Per year: 2,01,006 / 4 years (2021-22 to 2024-25) = about 50,000   [Estimate]
      Average central assistance per GIA beneficiary:
      Rs 1,219.80 cr / 2,01,006 = about Rs 60,700                      [Estimate]
      Share of the eligible pool helped each year: 50,000 / 5.9 crore
      = 0.085% -> fewer than 1 in 1,000 eligible people a year          [Estimate]
      -> Seats are scarce, so every seat must go to the right person.
1.7 Skilling inside GIA: Rs 457.82 crore released for 8,146 projects, including
      987 skill-development projects, in 2023-24 and 2024-25.  [Fact, PIB explainer, 1 May 2025]
1.8 Asset support: up to Rs 50,000 or 50% of the asset cost, whichever is less
      (raised from Rs 10,000), paid towards a loan.                       [Fact, PIB]
1.9 Expected SkillCall users (national)                                  [Estimate]
      ~50,000 GIA seats a year x 2-3 applicants screened per seat
      = 1.0 - 1.5 lakh profiles a year.

3. COST OF ONE COMPLETED CALL (per person profiled)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Unit prices
    Phone line (outbound to mobile) ... Rs 0.80 - 1.80 per minute        [Fact, 2026 Indian CPaaS rates]
    30-second billing pulses .......... +10% rounding                     [Estimate]
    Speech-to-text (Sarvam Saaras v3) . Rs 30 per hour = Rs 0.50/min      [Fact, sarvam.ai/api-pricing]
    Text-to-speech (Sarvam Bulbul v3) . Rs 3 per 1,000 characters         [Fact, sarvam.ai/api-pricing]
    LLM: Sarvam-30B blended ~US$0.03 per 1M tokens    [Fact, Artificial Analysis 2026]
         Sarvam-105B Rs 29.28 / 1M input, Rs 73.20 / 1M output tokens  [Fact, sarvam.ai/api-pricing]
    SMS (DLT) ......................... Rs 0.15 - 0.25 each               [Estimate, market rates]
    Human counsellor .................. Rs 25,000/month = ~Rs 3.2/min
                                        (22 days x 6 productive hours)   [Estimate]
3.2 Assumptions (low-cost case / high-cost case)                        [Estimate]
    Missed calls answered within 3 tries ... 76% (Kilkari) / 70%
    Finish the interview ................... 80% / 75%
    Finish the options part ................ 85% / 80%
    -> completed profiles per 1,000 missed calls: 517 / 420
    Minutes per completed profile: 10 (interview + options) + 6 (3 follow-up
      calls x 2 min at 1, 3, 6 months) = 16 min
    Unfinished callers: 1.5 min each (their cost is spread over completed ones)
    Unanswered ring time is not billed (standard CPaaS billing).
    Share of call time that is his speech (sent to speech-to-text): 50% / 60%
      (keypad answers and our own prompts need no speech-to-text)
    Pilot: all speech-to-text on the paid API (no GPU yet)
    Spoken text generated live: 800 / 2,000 characters (fixed questions are
      pre-recorded once and cost nothing after that)
    LLM: ~90% of turns on Sarvam-30B, ~10% escalated to 105B -> Rs 0.25 / 0.50
    SMS: 4 / 6 messages
    Human call-back: 10% of people x 5 min / 20% x 6 min
3.3 Result per completed profile (pilot)                     LOW       HIGH
    Phone line ............................................  Rs 14.7   Rs 33.7
      check: (517 x 16 + 243 x 1.5) min x Rs 0.80 x 1.10 / 517 = Rs 14.7
    Speech -> text .........................................  Rs  4.2   Rs  5.1
    Text -> speech .........................................  Rs  2.4   Rs  6.0
    AI brain (30B, 105B only when needed) ..................  Rs  0.25  Rs  0.5
    SMS ....................................................  Rs  0.6   Rs  1.5
    Human call-back ........................................  Rs  2.0   Rs  6.0
    Hosting & helpline number (pilot) ......................  Rs  1.0   Rs  3.0
    TOTAL ..................................................  Rs 25     Rs 56
3.4 At national scale the router sends ~70% of speech to free self-hosted
    IndicConformer and hosting becomes a fixed cost -> variable cost
    Rs 21 - 49 per profile.                                              [Estimate]
    Cross-check: Rs 25-56 / 16 min = Rs 1.6-3.5 per call-minute all-in.

5. OFFICER TIME SAVED  (cross-checked with the AJAY app)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 What already exists: the AJAY mobile app (launched 26 May 2026) moves
    surveys and village plans from paper to digital, with geo-tagging, role-based
    login, photo uploads, notifications and dashboards; the PM-AJAY portal is the
    MIS for GIA beneficiary registration, skill training and employment tracking.
                                                      [Fact, PIB / All India Radio, 26 May 2026]
    -> Data entry is already digital, so we do NOT count data-entry time.
    -> What the AJAY app does not do: talk to the applicant in his language,
       match his skills to NSQF courses, or make follow-up calls. A person still
       has to do the interview and the follow-ups. Only that time is counted.
5.2 Assumptions                                                          [Estimate]
    Today, per beneficiary: in-person interview / counselling 30 min
      + 3 follow-up calls x 5 min = 45 min   (travel time NOT counted)
    With SkillCall: officer reviews a ready profile and approves = 7.5 min
    Saved: 37.5 min per beneficiary
5.3 Results
    Per officer handling 40 cases a month: 40 x 45 min = 30 h -> 40 x 7.5 min
      = 5 h -> about 25 hours saved every month
    India (beneficiaries only): 50,000 x 37.5 min = 31,250 h a year
      = 3,906 working days = about 18 full-time staff-years
    If every applicant is screened (2 per seat): + 50,000 x 30 min = 25,000 h
      -> about 56,000 h = about 32 staff-years
    (1 staff-year = 220 working days x 8 hours)

6. IMPACT NUMBERS ON SLIDE 5
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Jobs: per 1,000 trainees - 41% placed = 410 people; government norm 70%
    = 700 (MSDE Common Norms). Moving even to 50% = 90 more people working. [Estimate]
6.2 Staying in the job: trainees given two information sessions about the real
    job were 11 percentage points (18%) more likely to stay 5+ months, against a
    comparison average of 62% -> 62% to 73%. Effect seen in male trainees.
    Less-educated trainees were 3.9 points (21%) less likely to drop out
    (suggestive).                    [Fact, J-PAL summary of Chakravorty et al., J. Dev. Econ. 2024]
6.3 Money better used: if wrong matches drop in 5 of every 100 cases:
    5% x 50,000 = 2,500 people
    x Rs 50,000 (asset-support cap) = Rs 12.5 crore
    x Rs 60,700 (average support)   = Rs 15.2 crore
    -> Rs 12.5 - 15 crore a year, about 6 - 14 times SkillCall's national cost
       (Rs 1.1 - 2.1 crore)                                           [Estimate]

7. WILL IT ACTUALLY WORK?  SPEED, ACCURACY, SCALE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Speed of one spoken turn (from the moment he stops talking)          [Estimate]
    end-of-speech detection ............ ~0.5 s
    speech-to-text ...................... ~0.07 - 0.3 s  (Saaras ~70 ms processing)  [Fact, Sarvam docs]
    AI first word (Sarvam-30B) .......... ~0.3 - 1.0 s   (NVIDIA-Sarvam serving target:
                                          P95 time-to-first-token < 1 s)          [Fact, NVIDIA 2026]
    voice starts (Bulbul v3) ............ ~0.19 - 0.25 s (187 ms first byte; sub-250 ms) [Fact, sarvam.ai]
    network / telephony ................. ~0.1 - 0.2 s
    TOTAL ............................... about 1.2 - 2.2 s per spoken turn
    Keypad answers and pre-recorded questions skip speech-to-text, the AI and
    live voice, so those turns are instant.
    Sarvam's own guide: use Sarvam-30B for voice agents (lower latency); keep
    105B for multi-step reasoning - the same router design we use.  [Fact, Sarvam docs]
7.2 Accuracy
    Best Indian speech AI on real phone calls: word error <= 20% (top tier:
    Sarvam-Audio, Gemini-3-Pro, IndicConformer); tested on 36,691 speakers,
    15 languages; Bhojpuri/Maithili 4-5x worse than Hindi.  [Fact, Voice of India, arXiv 2604.19151]
    Our design: exact facts by keypad (no speech error), every spoken answer read
    back, low confidence -> simpler question -> bigger model -> human.
    Residual error on spoken fields: 20% error x 10% missed at read-back = 2%;
    worst case 30% x 20% = 6%.                                          [Estimate]
    Low-literacy users: 0% finished a text task, 72% finished it by voice.
                                                        [Fact, Medhi et al., ACM TOCHI 2011]
7.3 Scale already proven in India
    UIDAI runs Sarvam's voice AI for Aadhaar in 10 languages, on-premise.  [Fact, PIB/MeitY 2025]
    Kilkari handles 1.2 million automated calls a day; 50% answer on the 1st
    try, 76% by the 3rd, 99.5% by the 9th.                    [Fact, BMJ Global Health 2021]
    CSCs: 5.58 lakh functional, 4.44 lakh at gram-panchayat level (31 Oct 2023). [Fact, MeitY, Lok Sabha]
    SHGs: 10.05 crore households in 90.90 lakh groups.                        [Fact, PIB]
